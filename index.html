SpiroPop

Roll the gear. Watch it bloom.

A calm spiral-art toy for all ages. Drag the gear around the ring and a glowing pattern draws itself, loop by loop, until it closes with a soft chime. 22 stages, 6 pen holes, 7 inks, Neon and Street styles, and no timers or scores.
